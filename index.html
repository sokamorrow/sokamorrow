REPUBLIC ACT NO. 11314

Student Fare Discount Act

Enacted on April 17, 2019, institutionalizing the grant of a statutory twenty percent (20%) discount on domestic public transportation utilities for Filipino students.

Statutory Discount: 20% off approved regular fare
Coverage: Elementary, secondary, technical-vocational, and undergraduate students
Validity: Everyday, including weekends, summer breaks, and legal holidays
Required Credential: Valid school ID or validated current enrollment receipt
Official Gazette Citation: Republic Act No. 11314
REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9994

Expanded Senior Citizens Act of 2010

Grants resident Filipino senior citizens aged sixty (60) years and above a mandatory 20% discount and Value Added Tax (VAT) exemption on public land transportation fares.

Statutory Discount: 20% off approved regular fare
Eligible Beneficiaries: Filipino citizens aged 60 years old and above
Required Credential: OSCA Senior Citizen ID or any government-issued ID showing date of birth
Official Gazette Citation: Republic Act No. 9994
REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7277 / RA 10754

Magna Carta for Persons with Disability (PWD)

Provides equal rights and privileges for persons with disabilities, mandating at least a 20% fare discount on all public transport services throughout the Philippines.

Statutory Discount: At least 20% off approved regular fare
Eligible Beneficiaries: Persons with registered physical, sensory, or mental disabilities
Required Credential: Valid PWD ID issued by the PDAO, City/Municipal Social Welfare, or NCDA
Official Gazette Citation: Republic Act No. 10754
LTFRB MC NO. 2017-024

Continuous Grant of Student Discounts

Reiterates that public utility vehicle operators and drivers must grant the 20% student fare discount daily throughout the entire calendar year.

Implementation: Effective 7 days a week, including weekends and holidays
Enforcement: Refusal to grant is a franchise violation subject to fines and CPC penalties
LTFRB Official Portal Verification
LTFRB TARIFF GUIDELINES

Quarter-Peso Rounding Policy

Specifies the official tariff formula and rounding methodology applied to computed public utility jeepney fares.

Base Distance: Applies to the first four (4) kilometers traveled
Succeeding Distance: Calculated per kilometer beyond the first 4 km
Rounding Standard: Officially rounded off to the nearest 25 centavos (₱0.25)
Mandatory Display: Official LTFRB Fare Matrix must be conspicuously posted inside the PUJ
